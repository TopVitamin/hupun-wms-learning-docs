#### 店铺授权
新增店铺时选取美团闪购类型

![1755686335326-1b825826-1017-4dc2-87c8-1ae9662a9afe.png](./img/JE_tVy1fDlBnbTIu/1755686335326-1b825826-1017-4dc2-87c8-1ae9662a9afe-396178.png)
